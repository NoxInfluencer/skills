# Cross-touchpoint facts and AI-answer integration experiment

Date: 2026-10-24

## Outcome and attempted change

Both attempted Skill versions were rejected. Manager and Expert are restored to
the baseline bytes. The retained work is synthetic cases 56/57, their existing
manual-review route, and this experiment record. Nine executions and 52
criterion/answer judgments preserve the initial limitations and the failed
narrow repair; this is not a successful integration or an all-outcomes pass.

Matching words across channels cannot establish matching product versions or
independent corroboration. Likewise, an accessible page, a brand mention and an
accurate buying recommendation answer different questions. The sixth material's
approved candidate methods were tested within the existing workflow:

- The operating-design candidate kept product identity, decision-relevant facts, dates or
  versions and use conditions coherent across chosen touchpoints. A dated,
  supported setup remains historical experience; it is not rewritten as current
  use. Official specifications, independent tests and personal use have different
  evidential roles. Copies of one release are one source.
- The playbook-8 candidate separated page access, mention/citation, factual accuracy and
  applicability, recommendation for the user's actual question, and business
  contribution. These are separate checks, not a required linear funnel.
  Relevant questions and the original platform/mode, time, answer and available
  source links make an observation reviewable.
- The same measurement candidate routed fact corrections to product/content
  owners and specific access-path gaps to the website owner. HTTP 200 and a raw
  JavaScript shell have limited evidential scope. Access policy is checked for
  its purpose; training permission does not establish search visibility.
  Investment claims use the existing attribution/incrementality method.

The attempted entrypoint extended the channel-interface route to shared SEO/GEO
content. Those changes and their synchronized Expert copy were withdrawn.
Existing claim review, feedback,
automation, data definitions and buying-reason methods remain the owners of
their respective guidance. No separate GEO module, crawler list, fixed survey
frequency, all-channel plan or website-deployment authority is introduced.
Original source identities, case numbers, screenshots and promotional content
remain local. New evaluation inputs are wholly synthetic.

## Fixed comparison and retention conditions

Before editing the Skill, the local protocol fixed cases 56/57 and existing
cases 38/48, their expectations, the original runtime and the retention policy.
Original cases 1–55 and supplied fixtures are preserved byte-for-byte or as
unchanged case objects. The adapter, configuration and its contract test only
extend the new ID lists; old outcome graders and the conversation runner are
unchanged. New cases use response evidence plus manual business review rather
than a keyword outcome grade or another model judge.

| Case | Fixed business question |
| --- | --- |
| 56 | Correct current compatibility facts while retaining dated, truthful experience, natural wording and source independence. |
| 57 | Interpret a branded mention, an inaccurate recommendation, limited access checks and related business observations; assign the smallest supported next checks. |
| 38 | Diagnose metric and attribution anomalies before expansion, retaining definitions and appropriate order/refund cohorts. |
| 48 | Preserve supported conditions, creator expression, truthful experience, CTA and disclosure in a concise content recommendation. |

The candidate must retain current factual conditions and actual observed state,
give useful corrections and owner/review handoffs, and avoid fabricated
independence, reliable coverage, causality, execution or authority. Ordinary
supported content preparation need not wait for every technical or causal check.
A material new commitment, launch or scope error requires rejection or a
separately justified narrow repair. Failed evidence is retained; preferred
answers do not justify reruns. An invalid infrastructure run permits one
identical recovery with the original error preserved.

Each case/variant uses an independent temporary workspace containing only its
declared evidence and the isolated Skill copies. Prompts, evidence, expectations
and runtime are shared; only Manager content differs. Expectations remain
outside the model request. Explicit Skill invocation tests loaded-Skill work,
not natural routing.

## Bounded repair and rejection

After the complete initial comparison, a separate local repair protocol kept
the same request, input, six expectations, runtime and original baseline. Only
one sentence was added: supported factual corrections should remain independent
of unresolved access or attribution checks. No criterion, input or outcome
grader was changed. One fresh candidate execution of case 57 ran from
`2026-10-24T15:37:57Z` to `2026-10-24T15:39:15Z`; no infrastructure recovery or
preferred-answer rerun was used.

The repaired answer allowed the fact/content work to proceed independently,
supporting criterion 6, but introduced a decisive source-fidelity error. The
packet says R1 has no supported waterproof claim. The proposed controlled fact
statement instead says `透气鞋面、非防水` (breathable upper, not waterproof).
Absence of supporting waterproof evidence does not establish the opposite
performance claim. Criterion 2 fails the original factual retention condition.
Criteria 1/4/5 remain partial: A's branded non-browsing observation is incompletely
retained, market/language context is incomplete, and the incremental-design
decision/cost tradeoff is still absent. Access/policy boundaries remain supported.

Both candidate versions were withdrawn under the fixed retention policy. The
six Manager files and synchronized Expert snapshot match the baseline exactly.
The new case coverage remains because it exposes source/version, independence,
observation-scope and correction-handoff failures without encoding a preferred
answer as a keyword grade. No further instruction expansion or model rerun was
used to turn this record green.

## Validation and remaining limits

Local checks pass for 57 canonical cases, validator/adapter self-tests,
46 Python tests, five Node tests, Promptfoo configuration, Skill validation and
Expert synchronization. Original cases 1–55, prior fixtures, outcome graders,
runner and runtime settings are preserved. The retained prepared fixture uses
the baseline Manager in both variants; historical candidate manifests remain
unchanged in the experiment archive. Links, public boundaries, whitespace and
restoration identities are checked before commit.

Nine completed executions and 52 reviewed criterion/answer pairs establish the
bounded experiment and rejection. They do not establish stable comparative
performance, natural routing, actual crawling/indexing, reliable recommendations,
live business execution or commercial lift. One observed defect is not proof
that the candidate always fails, but the fixed factual retention bar was not met.
The unconfirmed-experience issue is still open; the record does not relabel the
candidate's stronger six-hour transfer as harmless or previously identical.

The sixth material's source audit and experiment are complete; its proposed
general Skill expansion is not retained. Original source notes, exact source
statistics and the main extraction plan stay ignored. The seventh material has
not been started. This phase commits coverage and evidence, not a successful
method integration or a repaired marketing capability.
