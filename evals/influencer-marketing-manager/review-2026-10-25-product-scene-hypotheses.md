## Withdrawal, validation and limits

No repair or additional model run was attempted. The observed issue is output
fidelity to an existing decision rule; adding more general guidance would not
be an evidence-backed repair. The exact rejected candidate, original contract,
inputs, complete responses and failed criterion remain archived locally.

All six Manager files and the synchronized Expert snapshot match the baseline.
The active prepared fixtures were refreshed after restoration and also match
the retained Skill; the historical comparison manifest remains unchanged in
the experiment archive. Checks pass for 59 canonical cases, validator/adapter
self-tests, 46 Python tests, five Node tests, Promptfoo configuration, Skill
structure, Expert synchronization, unchanged original cases/fixtures, links and
public boundaries. Those structural checks do not turn the rejected behavior
into a pass.

The observation-window omissions, partial product-retest design and unconfirmed
ongoing-handling wording remain visible. No live marketing action, product
change, PMF, paid demand, causal lift or stable model reliability was verified.
The eighth material's bounded integration experiment is complete; no additional
Skill instruction is retained. Its source notes and the original extraction
plan remain ignored. The ninth material is not started by this phase.
