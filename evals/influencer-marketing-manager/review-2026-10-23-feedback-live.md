# Product-feedback and live-content review

Date: 2026-10-23

## Change and scope

The existing Manager could report product problems, select useful demonstrations
and arrange content reuse. Two short method passages now make the missing
connections explicit:

- In playbook 7.4, recurring feedback keeps its original problem, scene and
  source. Independent reports differ from copied discussion; severity, purchase
  impact and feasible handling matter more than volume alone. The product or
  service owner assesses handling, and a change is checked against the original
  problem. Planned, implemented and verified states stay separate. Public
  explanations retain confirmed facts and limits; co-creation requires actual
  participation. Useful guidance can resolve a problem without redesign.
- In operating-design's existing strategy section, live content starts from
  buyer questions and an appropriate demonstration or supported answer. Clips
  and FAQs retain important conditions and unanswered items, with an owner,
  next check and purpose-specific success signal. A live demonstration does not
  establish a long-term result, and clip length serves the explanation.

Two short examples clarify existing methods: a professional user's finished
work plus real workflow, and a useful task worth recording at an experience
event. Neither requires a community, contest, event-management or employee
creator program. Cultural-IP selection and continuous insight reuse existing
fit, rights, channel and measurement guidance.

The entrypoint adds live-content planning and recurring product feedback to
existing reference-reading routes. Claim review, rights, incrementality and
commitment authority remain owned by their existing guidance. The Expert's
bundled Skill is synchronized. No new module, fixed clip duration, universal
test protocol or external execution permission is introduced.

All evaluation products, records and figures are fictional. Original materials,
identities, screenshots, vendor-product demonstrations and source statistics
remain in the ignored local workspace; none are copied into public fixtures.

Cases 52/53 and their review expectations were frozen before editing the Skill.
All original 51 cases, including follow-ups and expectations, are unchanged.
The existing outcome graders are unchanged; the new cases use response evidence
and manual business review, with no keyword outcome grade or new model judge.

The two new methods must support these key judgments without blocking supported
guidance or demonstrations. Existing regressions must have no material new
error in acceptance, selected-use value, claim scope or proportionate correction.
Missing source reads are discovery findings, not proof of method validation.
Actual answers are reviewed by the same criteria on both sides; baseline success
is not candidate improvement. A failed condition permits one evidence-based
narrow correction or rollback, with earlier observations retained.

Each case/variant uses a fresh temporary directory containing the copied Skills
and only its declared inputs. Requests, evidence, runtime and review criteria
match; only Manager content differs. Expectations are not sent to the model.
Raw events, responses, source-read evidence and fixture identities remain local.

## Reproduction

Use the pinned dependencies and an existing Codex login from the repository root:

```bash
npm run eval:manager:prepare -- --baseline-ref d3cf755 --reuse-codex-login
npm run eval:manager:conversations -- --cases 52,53,46,47 \
  --variants baseline,candidate \
  --output evals/influencer-marketing-manager/workspace/source-4-<new-run>.json
```

Use a new output filename. The runner refuses to overwrite observations and
checks snapshot freshness before and after execution. Completion and zero exit
status establish response evidence, not business quality.

## Retention decision and limits

Retain the two method passages, two examples and narrow reading routes. The
candidate supports the core state, claim and permission boundaries while
preserving useful guidance and scoped demonstrations. The selected existing
cases do not establish a material new regression. No additional candidate
revision, grader change, outcome-driven rerun or new workflow is needed.

The retest plan and measurement handoffs still need project-specific completion;
case 46's historical handoff limitation remains open. This revision does not
repair full-Brief fidelity, establish industry benchmarks or prove unattended
execution. Source reading and eight completed responses are distinct from
business sufficiency. A single observation per case/variant cannot establish
stable improvement, natural routing reliability, product efficacy or commercial
lift. The baseline already handles much of the task; its successes and failures
remain visible under the same review criteria.
