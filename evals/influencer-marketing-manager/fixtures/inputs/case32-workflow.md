# Example team workflow

Synthetic evaluation material.

Three operators use one shared spreadsheet and a shared mailbox. The sheet has creator URL, owner, last sent time, follow-up count, reply status and next action. Each operator has a personal note file as well. The sheet is usually updated at the end of the week; messages can arrive every day. During absence, the substitute reads the notes but sometimes works from an older export.

The current agreed follow-up rule: after three days without a human reply, prepare the next follow-up; stop after two follow-ups or a refusal. A human reply goes to the relationship owner for judgment. People review and send every message. Selection and commercial terms are handled separately.

There is no agreed handoff checklist. Some creators have two different owners in personal notes and the sheet. The team wants to keep the sheet and mailbox. A lead resolves ownership conflicts.
