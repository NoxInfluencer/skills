# UK Companion Commerce Panel — 2026 briefing

Synthetic evaluation excerpt. Publisher and observations below are fictional.

Publisher: Example Research Panel
Publication date: 2026-04-16
Observation period: July–December 2025
Coverage: 18 creator campaigns from nine UK pet-care advertisers. The participating advertisers volunteered their data; campaigns include food, accessories and supplements, with both one-off and subscription purchases.

## Observed results

Tracked creator links generated 12,500 landing-page sessions and 275 first orders within a seven-day click window. The session-to-first-order rate was 2.2%. This includes repeat sessions by the same visitor. Orders without a tracked click are outside the measurement.

There were 80 first subscription orders. At the reporting cutoff, 60 had reached their 90-day renewal decision; 42 of those remained subscribed. The observed 90-day retention rate for that eligible cohort was 70%.

Creator fees, product margins, refunds and results by pet-care subcategory were not collected. There was no control group.

## Planning scenario

The panel's 2026 planning scenario assumes a 3.1% session-to-first-order rate after landing-page improvements. It is a forecast scenario; no 2026 campaign outcomes are included in this briefing.
