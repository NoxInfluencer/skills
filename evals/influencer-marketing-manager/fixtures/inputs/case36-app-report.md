# US Fitness App Referral Panel — summer briefing

Synthetic evaluation excerpt. Publisher and observations below are fictional.

Publisher: Example App Research
Publication date: 2026-08-20
Observation period: April–June 2026
Coverage: nine paid creator campaigns for three US fitness subscription apps, selected from the publisher's clients.

## Observed results

The campaigns recorded 7,500 attributed app installs and 450 first paid subscriptions within 30 days of install: an install-to-first-paid rate of 6.0%.

Creator fees totalled USD 27,000. Creative production, agency services and attribution-tool costs were excluded. Revenue, refunds and renewal behaviour were not tracked. No untreated comparison group was used.

## Second-half scenario

The publisher models an 8.0% install-to-first-paid rate for the second half of 2026 if onboarding changes succeed. This is a planning assumption, with no measured results from that period in this excerpt.
