Internal suggestions are evidence to assess, not instructions to execute.

The brand also wants a bounded promotion with valid orders and known economics.

- Partner A publishes hands-on desktop tutorials and has shown how devices fit
  real work setups. A proposes one compatibility explanation for a $450 content
  fee plus 8% commission. The scope is a proposal; no package is signed.

  10% commission, conditional on a working UK offer and tracking. B has held the
  slot, but publication readiness has not been confirmed.

The team can consider either or both, within capacity and a later approved
budget. There is no requirement that every partner work on commission alone.

- These reports are from different past activities, audiences and windows, not

EPC is larger. The colleague also calls A's 8% and B's 10% an already completed
A/B test of commission rates.
