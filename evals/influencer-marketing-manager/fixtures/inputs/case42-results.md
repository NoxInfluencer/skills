# Synthetic measurement snapshot

All amounts are GBP. The acquisition report covers the first ten days after publication. A 20,000-follower channel received 80,000 organic video views; no paid promotion was booked. There is no supplied bot or fraud evidence.

The analytics export reports 1,500 page views, 1,000 distinct visitors identified by its first-party visitor ID, and 250 network IP addresses. Its definitions allow shared IPs and repeated page views. The 1,000 visitors are attributed to the campaign link in this same ten-day cohort.

The order export links 30 GBP 30 first orders to this cohort. Six of those orders were fully refunded by the snapshot. Finance shows ten refunds processed in the period: those six plus four refunds belonging to an earlier campaign. The earlier refunds total GBP 80. Some current visitors may still order later; there is no mature renewal or lifetime-value data.

Confirmed campaign costs are GBP 600 creator fee, GBP 60 sample and GBP 40 delivery. Contribution margin and internal labor cost are unavailable. Customer support reports some visitors saw a discount mismatch and unexpected shipping charges; frequency and financial effect have not been measured.

The finance summary subtracts all ten refunds from the 30 orders and labels the result “20 net campaign orders.” A colleague suggests comparing UV and IP for equality and treating views above followers as evidence of fraud.
