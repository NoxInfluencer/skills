# Booking-tool growth snapshot

All products, people, records and figures are fictional evaluation inputs.
This packet supplies evidence; it does not authorize execution or publication.

## CURRENT — Product and intended users

- The current S1 booking tool is version 2.3. The approved next-pilot audience is
  small coaching studios that schedule sessions across several coaches.
- The product lead's current capability record says version 2.3 supports a
  personal calendar only. It does not support multi-coach scheduling. There is
  no accepted multi-coach implementation or release date.
- The creator tutorial and landing page show an individual coach arranging
  personal sessions. Neither demonstrates studio scheduling.

## OBSERVED — What the records show

- The website report contains 180 campaign-tagged visitors over fourteen days.
  The product export has 24 trial accounts, with trial ages from two to fourteen
  days. A visitor-to-account identity mapping has not been reconciled.
- Five accounts have a personal-calendar task-completion event. Those events
  do not show a completed multi-coach task or continued use.
- The billing export has two paid accounts, both independent solo coaches.
  Their acquisition sources are not established; neither is a studio account.
- Four interviewed trial users operate studios and ask about multi-coach
  scheduling. Three supplied session recordings: two did not invite another
  coach and one looked for team scheduling but could not find it. These four
  interviews do not establish the needs of all visitors or the reason for every
  unconverted trial. The other trial accounts' intent is unknown.
