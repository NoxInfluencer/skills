# Synthetic delivery incidents

The user requests recommendations only. Three partnerships have separate agreements:

- A: a high-value sample arrived with a faulty mounting part. The creator supplied photos and says the product cannot be demonstrated as planned. A deposit has been paid and the launch is five days away. The brand has not confirmed replacement stock or shipping time. The creator asks for a working sample or cancellation.
- B: a video was published and is now unavailable. The contract requires six months of public availability and a reasonable correction if content is removed. The creator forwarded a platform notice about an unsupported product claim; there is no evidence that the creator voluntarily deleted it. The copy containing the claim was supplied by the brand.
- C: the agreed video and links remain public, with fewer views than hoped. No view guarantee, free cross-post, paid-use permission or additional budget was agreed. The creator offers to discuss an extra placement and paid usage.
