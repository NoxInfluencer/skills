# Manager improvement plan

Updated: 2026-10-22

## Outcome and lessons

The useful outcome is a supported next marketing decision or authorized action
with less material correction. Reading a reference, producing a polished answer,
completing a model turn and passing a packaging check establish different things.

The three source integrations remain organized around the actual operating
workflow: goal and contribution, renewal or reuse decision, creative evidence,
then the next check. Preserve that structure and the conditional methods. A
source's numerical example, one project's terms or one failed answer must not
become a universal operating requirement.

## Fixed Brief experiment

Hypothesis: preserving an accepted term's object, responsible party and condition
through a compact working note, followed by a comparison with the original
agreement, can reduce lossy Brief drafting. This is a testable hypothesis, not a
diagnosis of the model's internal cause or an assurance that a note will work.

Replace the generic duration reminder with this working method in the existing
delivery passage. Make accepted cooperation terms explicit in the existing Brief
outline, whose current four blocks omit a place for the creator fee. Present the
outline as relevant content rather than a required format. Reuse existing
agreement records; do not require a new schema, tool, user-visible form or a full
contract for preliminary discussion. The source comparison must not rely only
on the model's own extracted note.

Before the candidate changes, keep these requests and qualitative criteria fixed:

| Case | Purpose | Material things to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 41, existing two turns | Reproduce the known Brief problem and check downstream review. | Integration duration versus host video, GBP 800, payment after publication, brand review responsibility, supported claims, truthful criticism, product-team investigation before release and excluded extra scope. |
| 51, new single turn | Test transfer to a different product and multi-deliverable agreement. | Whole video versus segment versus Story duration, EUR 1,200 and payment trigger, separate review/revision owners and time units, publication/retention, exclusions and no invented completion. |
| 25, unchanged single turn | Check proportionality in a preliminary client decision. | Known proposal and material terms stay visible; missing later terms do not prevent useful direction, and no full-contract gate or internal worksheet replaces the client summary. |

Only Manager may differ. Both variants use the current case contract, shared
Skill and runtime in separate workspaces; later case 41 input remains absent
from its opening turn. Review criteria are not passed to the model. Keep full
answers and failed observations, and record fingerprints and execution counts.

Review each known, decision-relevant obligation as a source/answer pair. Check
the value together with its object, owner, trigger and conditions where supplied.
Judge an omission or changed relationship explicitly; number presence alone is
insufficient. Do not require a fixed customer-facing checklist or all possible
contract fields. Use the same standard for both variants.

Retain the candidate only if this comparison supports removal of case 41's
material Brief defects, preserves the corresponding obligations in case 51,
and introduces no material regression in case 25 or case 41's later review.
Runtime or source-isolation failures invalidate the affected comparison. A
single paired observation may support retention; it does not establish stability,
causal attribution, user acceptance or production readiness.

Run one candidate version once per selected case and variant. If it does not
meet the retention rule, restore the previous Manager bytes and retain the probe
and failure record. Do not tune the new example or rerun until it happens to
pass. Any later artifact or tool intervention needs a concrete failure it can
prevent and a separate bounded test.
