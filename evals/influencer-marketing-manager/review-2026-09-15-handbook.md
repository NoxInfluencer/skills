## Source decisions

The 19-page experience deck and 73-page operations manual were read page by page; the image-based CPM table was also checked visually. The retained value is the reasoning behind the work:

- Choose formats from audience needs and the demonstration required. Expand searches from product and scene terms to problems, adjacent interests and comparable collaborations.
- Compare current content of the required format, including sponsored work. Keep creator fit separate from contact readiness.
- Bind prices to scope, currency, payment, rights and timing. Separate agency availability from creator acceptance, and gifting from an agreed product-for-content exchange.
- Confirm material requirements before agreement. Build a short Brief, review against it with concrete edits, and preserve truthful creator experience.
- Separate sample failure, platform removal and weak performance when choosing a remedy. Carry repeated problems back into the project SOP.
- Reconcile metric definitions, attribution windows, order cohorts, refunds and complete cost before changing budget.

Fixed platform statistics, quoted prices, CPM tables, conversion rates, payment percentages and regional generalizations were not adopted as operating rules. CPM means cost per thousand impressions; a view-based calculation needs its own label. UV and IP definitions can produce different counts, and recommendation traffic can produce views above follower count. Neither observation alone establishes fraud.
