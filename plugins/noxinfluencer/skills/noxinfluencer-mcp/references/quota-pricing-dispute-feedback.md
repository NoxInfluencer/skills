# Quota And Operation Credit Costs

Use `quota` for remaining Skill Credit and authoritative execution access. A positive balance does not grant permission to execute a tool. Distinguish insufficient credits, missing execution permission and a separate feature limit using the service's error code and confirmed facts.

Use `list_tool_pricing` only with an explicit `tool` or `action` for the operation being considered. Explain its unit and unit credit cost. Use an existing operation-specific preflight when available; without the required quantities and confirmed billing basis, leave the estimated total unknown. Do not present a standard page cost as the current operation's total.

MCP does not offer membership plans, trial claims, upgrades, recharges or purchases. Do not call a membership-pricing tool, construct commercial links or open a membership page to resolve a restriction. Explain the limitation neutrally and preserve completed business results. Report allowance or costs only when requested or when a restriction blocks the task.

Prices and entitlements are server-owned. A successful action can depend on both Skill Credit and a separate product entitlement. Do not infer current allowance from historical usage or treat credit exhaustion as an OAuth failure.
