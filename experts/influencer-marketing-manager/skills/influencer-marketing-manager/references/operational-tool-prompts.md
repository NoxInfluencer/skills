# Operational Tool Prompts

Use these prompts when a routine partnership task benefits from a focused external observation or artifact. Connect the result to the current stage result and next business action.

| Tool | What it helps with | Prompt when | Result to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [17TRACK](https://www.17track.net/zh-hk/) | Global package tracking | A sample has shipped and the team needs to check transport, receipt, or a possible delay | Shipment progress and exceptions that inform reminders, schedule changes, replacement, or continued production |
| [Amazon Logistics / OrderTracker](https://www.ordertracker.com/couriers/amazon) | Amazon Logistics and TBA-style package lookup | An Amazon delivery needs a supplementary status check or the primary logistics view is incomplete | The package status viewed alongside the cooperation timeline and the next fulfillment decision |
| [Sign.Plus online signature](https://www.sign.plus/zh/online-signature-generator/type-signature) | A typed signature image for an ordinary document | A document needs a visual signature style for preparation or presentation | The prepared document artifact; formal agreement completion follows the team's approved signing record |
| [YouTube To Text](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-to-text/apnedodbofogffiagpekmbeflilkcbgf?hl=zh-CN) | YouTube text and time-coded transcript | A creator passes coarse screening and recent videos need a faster content and scene review | Time-stamped content clues about use scenes, claims, commercial context, and brand-safety questions, combined with the video and channel evidence |

## Prompt shape

Use one concise sentence in the form:

```text
当前阶段 + 业务问题 + 工具 + 该结果支持的判断
```

Examples:

- 样品已寄出，先用 17TRACK 看是否出现延误，再决定是否调整拍摄时间。
- 这个达人进入精选阶段，可以用 YouTube To Text 快速理解近期代表视频，再结合画面确认真实场景。

These tools are operator aids in the workflow. Record an observation or artifact only when it is available and relevant to the current decision.
