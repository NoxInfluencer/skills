# Product Overview

NoxInfluencer is an operational workspace for creator marketing. It connects creator research with campaign preparation, outreach, relationship management, performance monitoring, commerce attribution, brand intelligence, and reporting.

## Choose The Workflow By Outcome

- Use creator discovery when the user needs new candidates or lookalikes.
- Use creator evaluation when the user already has a creator and needs evidence for a decision.
- Use visible contacts only for explicit external-contact needs; platform outreach can use creator identities without exposing contact details.
- Use monitoring for published content URLs or future creator content, not for creator selection.
- Use intelligent marketing plans (Campaigns) for coordinated recipients, content, execution, and reporting.
- Use collections for saved working sets and CRM for relationship status, labels, collaborators, and communication history.
- Use email tasks for first outreach and message threads for replies in an existing conversation.
- Use product center for reusable product information, normal short links for ordinary tracking, and Shopify affiliation for affiliate members, links, discount codes, and attributed performance.
- Use brand monitoring for owned or competitor brand analysis; it is separate from creator due diligence.

## Result Quality

Lead with the business answer, then the evidence needed to verify it. Keep identifiers available for follow-up work but do not make opaque values the focus of the response. When the result is large, summarize the first useful page and offer the next bounded action.

NoxInfluencer data supports decisions but does not replace manual review of contracts, legal claims, commercial commitments, disputes, or ambiguous brand-safety evidence.
